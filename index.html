Model & assumptions

Semi-Empirical Educational Simulator

  • Measured level is a trait: a measured starting 25(OH)D is explained by a persistent individual factor (dose response, or effective sun + diet) fitted so your own inputs sustain it at the start date's season. It does not wash out.
  • Skin plateau: previtamin D3 approaches a photo-equilibrium within about one minimal erythemal dose, so extra time in strong sun adds little. Darker skin and sunscreen slow the approach; low winter sun lowers the plateau itself (vitamin D winter).
  • Body size: volumes and clearances scale with body weight (volumetric dilution), so the same input gives a lower level in a larger body.
  • Nonlinear metabolism: saturable 25-hydroxylation (CYP2R1, matters for large boluses) and CYP24A1 induction (clearance rises with 25(OH)D).
  • Clear-Sky UV Index: solar input from latitude, date, time, altitude and cloud cover.
WARNING: This is an educational tool and has not been fully externally validated against regulatory datasets. Parameters are population-average approximations. Do not use this tool to plan supplementation.
