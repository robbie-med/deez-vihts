High-Fidelity PBPK-Lite Architecture

  • Enzyme Kinetics: 25-hydroxylation uses saturable Michaelis-Menten kinetics (Vmax/Km) rather than linear clearance, correctly modeling how levels plateau during summer or high supplementation.
  • Dynamic Catabolism: Explicitly models CYP24A1 induction via calcitriol feedback, causing the 25(OH)D half-life to dynamically shorten as levels rise.
  • Adipose Tissue: Replaces arbitrary fractional transfer with physiological perfusion-limited exchange based on cardiac output fraction and lipid partition coefficients.
  • PTH/Calcitriol Axis: Calcitriol is now a fully dynamic state variable with production stimulated by a quasi-steady-state Parathyroid Hormone (PTH) proxy that responds to a simulated calcium index.
  • Skin Photochemistry: Simulates the thermal isomerization of Previtamin D3 to D3 alongside UV-driven photodegradation to lumisterol/tachysterol, preventing infinite synthesis.
  • Environmental: Extends solar equations to account for cloud cover attenuation, sunscreen SPF, and altitude.
Educational model, not medical advice. Parameters are population-average approximations; do not use this tool to plan supplementation.
